Every act starts the same way: you stare at the map, trace routes with your finger, and try to hold four of them in your head at once.

This mod does that part for you. Click a node ahead of you to pin it, and it draws every route that reaches your pins, in the game's own hand-drawn style. Pin the elite you want, the fire you need after it, and see which ways through the act actually give you both.

Features include:

- Pin any node ahead of you to see the routes that reach it. Pin several to narrow them down; double-click a node to pin every node of that type at once (all the elites, all the fires).
- Pins are candidates, not constraints. Routes are ranked by how many of your pins they reach, so when your pins disagree you still see the best options instead of an empty screen.
- A replacement Legend that counts: node types down the side, one column per route. Hover a type to light up every node of that type on the map, hover a route to preview it, select it to lock it in.
- Locked routes stay locked as you travel along them, and your pins survive closing the map - and closing the game.
- A Zoom button that cycles three views: the normal map, the whole act on one screen, and the whole act rotated on its side with the start at the left and the boss at the right.
- Full controller support. Right Trigger zooms; in the zoomed views the d-pad walks the map node by node with a cursor ring, and select pins whatever it lands on.
- A settings menu for manual path planning, marker size, and live sliders for the trail's look and the wide view's framing.

Informational only - the mod reads the map and draws over it. Nothing about the run changes, and a "?" node stays a "?".

Feedback and feature requests always welcome!
